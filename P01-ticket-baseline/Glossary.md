# Business glossary

| Term | Meaning in this project |
|---|---|
| Capacity | The maximum number of confirmed bookings one concert can have. In our examples it is 20. |
| Concert | One event on a specific date and time. Friday and Saturday are different concerts, each with its own capacity and bookings. |
| Confirmed booking | A saved record that gives one customer entry to one concert. It counts as confirmed when it is saved, even if the email has not arrived. |
| Sold out | The number of confirmed bookings for a concert has reached its capacity. No more bookings can be added for it. |
| Cancellation | A possible future action that removes a customer's booking. Not part of version one. When the ticket becomes available again is still unresolved. |
